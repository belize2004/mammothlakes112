# BRANDING-DNA.md — Mammoth Conestoga 112

## Purpose

This document defines the brand-design logic and aesthetic architecture for **Mammoth Conestoga 112** before layout, components, or code are created.

The goal is to elevate the website from an outdated generic vacation rental template to an authentic, high-elevation alpine sanctuary with direct OwnerRez booking integration.

Every page, layout, component, interaction, and copy decision must originate from this Brand DNA.

---

# 1. BRAND TRUTH

## What is fundamentally true about Mammoth Conestoga 112?

* **Authentic High-Sierra Roots:** Roger & Stephanie have journeyed to Mammoth together since 2000, got married at Mammoth Mountain’s 11,053-ft summit on leap year day in 2008, and adopted two dogs from the Big Pine shelter on a Sierra trip. Conestoga 112 is their personal, beloved mountain haven—not a corporate investment property.
* **Dialed-In End-Unit Layout:** A light-filled 612 sq. ft. studio + loft with vaulted ceilings, a cozy wood stove, a fully remodeled gourmet kitchen (2021), a newly renovated upstairs bath with walk-in shower (2024), and an ultra-convenient downstairs half-bath.
* **Unrivaled Location & Frictionless Access:** 
  * Less than 1 mile to The Village at Mammoth and the Village Gondola.
  * 0.5 miles to Canyon Lodge and 1 mile to Eagle Lodge.
  * Steps from the free mountain shuttle stop.
  * Dedicated front-door parking (2 reserved spaces) for painless winter gear loading and unloading.
* **Dog-First Mountain Living:** Built by passionate animal lovers who know the pain of finding quality dog-friendly mountain lodging. Equipped with a dedicated entryway paw station (Mud-Buster, dog towels, specialized mats, feeding bowls).
* **Proven Guest Satisfaction:** Over 257+ verified direct reviews with an aggregate **4.91 / 5.0 rating**.

### Brand Truth

> **Mammoth Conestoga 112 is a warm, thoughtfully appointed high-elevation mountain basecamp owned by lifelong Sierra adventurers—blending authentic hospitality, direct mountain access, and dog-friendly comfort at 8,000 feet.**

# 5. PRIMARY RECOGNITION CODES

## Code 1 — Topographic & Elevation Metadata Layering
*Rule:*
Anchor section headers and hero photography with crisp, precision monospaced coordinates, elevation markers, and registration badges:
`37.6435° N, 118.9936° W // ELEV. 8,050 FT // UNIT 112 // TOML-CPAN-11083`

---

## Code 2 — Chiseled Stone & Timber Hairline Framing
*Rule:*
Images and layout modules use sharp 0px to subtle 2px corner radii, paired with crisp hairline borders (`0.5px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` or warm stone hairlines). No rounded bubble cards, floating glassmorphism, or heavy drop shadows.

---

## Code 3 — Alpenglow Ember Accent
*Rule:*
A grounded foundation of Deep Basalt Charcoal (`#131518`) and Snow Alabaster (`#F7F5F0`), energized by a single electric warm ember accent (`#D95A2B` Sierra Alpenglow) reserved strictly for direct booking triggers, active calendar dates, and live status badges.

# 7. VISUAL GRAMMAR

## Rule 1 — One Dominant Mountain Viewport
Every section has one unambiguous visual hero—highlighting the expansive Sierra landscape, the warm wood stove interior, or the direct reservation calendar.

## Rule 2 — Photography Outweighs UI Chrome
High-resolution photography and authentic mountain light command 75%+ of the visual field. Controls and typography remain quiet and razor-sharp.

## Rule 3 — Monospace Utility Grounds Editorial Typography
Every expressive headline is paired with functional monospaced data (distance to lifts, square footage, bed sizes, Wi-Fi speed).

## Rule 4 — Asymmetric Negative Space
Uncluttered layouts with generous spacing that echo the vast openness of the Sierra Nevada.

## Rule 5 — Tactile Matte Over Digital Gloss
Deep matte dark tones, natural wood grain, snowy alabaster. Zero neon rainbow gradients, zero generic clipart.

# 8. TYPOGRAPHY DNA

## Display Typography
* **Direction:** Editorial Serif or Crisp Architectural Grotesk (*Fraunces* / *Playfair* / *PP Neue Montreal* / *Cabinet Grotesk*).
* **Qualities:** Chiseled, confident, grounded, warm.
* **Use for:** Hero statements, section titles, story headings.

## Body Typography
* **Direction:** Refined Humanist Sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter*).
* **Qualities:** Highly legible, calm, comfortable reading contrast.
* **Use for:** Property descriptions, amenity lists, host stories, FAQ, policies.

## Utility Typography
* **Direction:** Precision Monospace (*JetBrains Mono* / *Geist Mono*).
* **Use for:** Coordinates, permit # `TOML-CPAN-11083`, pricing rates `$125–$300/nt`, specs (`612 SQ FT`, `250+ MBPS`), timestamps.

* **Sierra Alpenglow Ember (`#D95A2B` / `#E26535`):** Reserved for primary conversion actions, available calendar dates, and key highlights.
